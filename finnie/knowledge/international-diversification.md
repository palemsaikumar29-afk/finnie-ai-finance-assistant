# International Diversification

The U.S. is roughly 60% of global market cap - leaving 40% abroad. International stocks diversify economic and currency exposure, and leadership rotates: non-U.S. markets outperformed in the 2000s while the U.S. dominated the 2010s. Home-country bias (overweighting your own market) feels comfortable but concentrates risk. A common approach: hold 20-40% of equities in international index funds. Currency moves add volatility short-term but wash out over decades. Emerging markets add growth potential with extra political and currency risk - size them modestly.

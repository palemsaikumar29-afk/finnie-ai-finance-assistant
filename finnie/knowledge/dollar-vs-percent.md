# Thinking in Dollars vs Percentages

A 50% loss requires a 100% gain just to break even - percentages are asymmetric, and this math punishes volatility. A portfolio that swings +30%/-30% alternately goes nowhere fast (geometric drag). This is why 'average returns' mislead: an investment up 100% then down 50% has a 25% arithmetic average but 0% actual growth. Always evaluate with compound (geometric) returns over full cycles, and remember that avoiding large losses matters more than capturing every rally - defense compounds too.

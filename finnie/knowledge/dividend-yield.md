# Dividend Yield and Payout Ratio

Dividend yield = annual dividends per share divided by share price. A 4% yield on a $100 stock pays $4/year. Yields rise when prices fall, so a soaring yield can signal distress, not bargains. The payout ratio (dividends divided by earnings) shows sustainability: 30-60% is typically healthy; above 80-100% leaves no cushion and cuts may follow. Compare yields within sectors, not across - utilities naturally yield more than tech. For income investors, dividend growth (rising payouts over time) often beats a high but stagnant starting yield.

# Behavioral Biases That Cost Investors Money

Humans are wired to invest badly. Key biases: loss aversion (losses hurt ~2x more than equivalent gains please, causing panic selling); recency bias (assuming recent trends continue); confirmation bias (seeking news that supports your holdings); overconfidence (trading too much); herding (buying manias, selling crashes); and anchoring (fixating on a past price). Defenses are structural: automation, written investment policy statements, diversified index funds, and pre-committed rebalancing rules that remove decisions from heated moments.

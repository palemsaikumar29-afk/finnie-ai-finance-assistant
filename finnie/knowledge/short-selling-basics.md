# Short Selling Basics

Short selling means borrowing shares, selling them, and hoping to buy back cheaper - profiting from declines. Risk is asymmetric: gains cap at 100% (stock to zero) while losses are theoretically unlimited, plus you pay borrow fees and can be forced to cover in short squeezes (see meme-stock episodes). It's a professional trading tool, not an investing strategy - most individuals should express negative views by simply not owning, or via defined-risk options. If you must, position-size tiny and use hard exit rules.

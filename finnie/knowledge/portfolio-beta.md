# Beta: Measuring Market Sensitivity

Beta measures how much an investment moves with the overall market. Beta of 1.0 moves with the market; 1.5 is 50% more volatile; 0.5 is half as twitchy; negative beta (rare) moves oppositely. Utilities and consumer staples typically have low betas; tech and small-caps run high. A portfolio's weighted-average beta tells you its market sensitivity - a 0.8-beta portfolio should fall ~16% in a 20% market drop. Beta says nothing about expected return by itself; pair it with alpha (excess return) and drawdown history for a fuller picture.

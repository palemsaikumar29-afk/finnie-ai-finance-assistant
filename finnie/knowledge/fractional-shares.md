# Fractional Shares

Fractional shares let you buy a slice of a stock or ETF with any dollar amount - $50 of a $500 stock buys 0.1 shares. This makes diversification possible with small balances and enables exact dollar-based investing and dividend reinvestment. Fractional holdings carry the same proportional economics as whole shares. Limitations: not all brokers offer them, transfers between brokers can force liquidation of fractions, and voting rights may not extend to fractional owners. For automated small-dollar investing, they're a genuine democratizing feature.

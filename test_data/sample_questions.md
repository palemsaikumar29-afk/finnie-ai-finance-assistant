# FINNIE — Sample Questions

Copy-paste any of these into the **Chat** tab to exercise each specialist
agent. Expected behavior for each is described in
[`expected_behavior.md`](expected_behavior.md).

> Tip: the caption under each assistant reply shows which agent handled it
> (e.g. `routed to 'news'`).

## Finance Q&A (`finance_qa`)

1. What is compound interest and why does starting early matter?
2. Explain the difference between an ETF and a mutual fund.
3. What is dollar-cost averaging? Is it better than investing a lump sum?
4. How does diversification actually reduce risk?

## Portfolio Analysis (`portfolio`)

1. Analyze my portfolio
2. How is my portfolio allocated? Which position is the largest?
3. What is my total P&L across all holdings?

## Market Analysis (`market`)

1. What is the price of AAPL?
2. Give me quotes for SPY, QQQ and NVDA
3. How is TSLA doing today?

## Goal Planning (`goals`)

1. I want $1 million in 20 years — how much do I need to save monthly?
2. I need $250k for a house down payment in 8 years
3. How much per month to reach $500k in 15 years?

## News Synthesizer (`news`)

1. What are the latest market headlines?
2. Latest news on the Fed and interest rates
3. Any recent news about NVIDIA?

## Tax Education (`tax_education`)

1. How are capital gains taxed?
2. Explain the wash-sale rule
3. What is tax-loss harvesting?
4. Traditional IRA vs Roth IRA — how do the tax treatments differ?

> The tax agent is **education only**. Every answer carries a disclaimer that
> it is not tax advice and recommends consulting a licensed tax professional.
